Game Ka Uddeshya:

Sahi habits (Defenders) ko place karke period symptoms (Enemies) ko rokna hai aur apna Wellness 100% maintain karna hai. Energy jama karein aur jarurat padne par defenders par tap karke unhe Upgrade karein!

Defenders (Aapki Takat):

Sahi enemy par sahi defender lagane se damage DOUBLE hota hai.

🔥

💡 Real Life: Jaise hot water bag ya heating pad pet aur kamar dard mein turant aaram dete hain.
🥬
Iron (Healthy Diet) Fatigue (😩) yaani thakan ko mitaye.
💡 Real Life: Jaise palak ya anar khane se body ko taqat milti hai aur susti door hoti hai.
💧
Hydration (Paani) Bloating (🎈) ko jaldi khatam kare.
💡 Real Life: Jaise khoob paani peene se body se toxins nikalte hain aur pet halka mehsus hota hai.
😴
Rest (Aaram) Mood Swings (🌪️) ko slow karke control kare.
💡 Real Life: Jaise ek achi neend lene ke baad mood fresh ho jata hai aur gussa shant hota hai.
🧘
Yoga (Vyayam) Ek sath 2 symptoms par area-damage kare.
💡 Real Life: Jaise halki stretching aur deep breathing se poori body ka stress ek sath kam hota hai.

Enemies (Symptoms):

🎈
Bloating Tez chalta hai, Hydration se harega.
💡 Real Life: Jab periods mein water retention ke karan pet phoola hua aur bhari lagta hai.
⚡

💡 Real Life: Jab achanak pet ya kamar ke nichle hisse mein tez dard aur marod uthti hai.
😩

Mood Swing Sabse tez enemy! Isko Rest lagakar slow karein.
💡 Real Life: Jab bina kisi wajah ke achanak gussa aana, chidchidapan ya rone ka mann karta hai.
👹
PMS Wave (Boss) Har 4th wave me aata hai. Bahut powerful hota hai!
💡 Real Life: Periods shuru hone se theek pehle ka samay jab saari pareshaniyan ek sath peak par hoti hain.
